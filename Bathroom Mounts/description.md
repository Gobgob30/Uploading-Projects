This is an assortment of mounts for various bathroom items.

All are installed and recommended to be installed with 3M VHB tape, others can be used, an have been but the performace has and can be sub par.

### _Install instruction:_ 1: Lay Tape 2: Cut Tape 3: Remove tape backing 4: _STICK IT GOOD._

Images are a mix of current and previous versions, newest ones are the only ones uploaded, most things have a better settup to cutout, the old ones were pain.

1. Brushknocker: The idea is to knock the shtuff out the brush. It has kinda just became a holder for my phone, not exactly recommended but.... Turns out the jet setting does enough lol.

2. Handled Holder: Standard holder for toothbrushes and things that are handled, IE the tongue scraper. Has a mesh/perforated bottom for drainage

3. Toothpaste Holder: Same idea as previous but with a large enough diameter to hold toothpaste.

4. Hooks: Just a simple hook, designed to accept simple metal hook or nice ones like pictured. Has ball ends for the simple hooks if used, just glue on.

5. Razor Holder: The oldest one, it is made to hold a single bladed razor and its blade. I noticed my blade would rust even when thoroughly cleaned, so I decided to remove the element that caused it. Just take the blade out, set it on the side holder\*, and use the locking mechanism to hold it into the slot. \*Make sure it doesn't touch the side, it will rust there.... Needs a mild redesign at some point, I will get to it.
