# Picking Additions that make this tolerable.

## QR Tokens 1-8

Dead simple tokens that simply sit on the edge of the tray or on the top of cart. They sit neatly in between squares.

If you have a vertical mount that allows distance between your phone and camera you can put it in the squares and chain items to the same tote. Just put item in front of camera, hit scan, move and bag, tote gets scanned.

Just know they are a pain for initial time cost, filling, sanding, and finishing takes a lot of time.

## Hangers

Just simple hooks that clamp on and allow you to put small items into the bags, single bagging items and minimizing moving around your cart.

### Required hardware

- 1x per hanger M3 nut, screw, washer
