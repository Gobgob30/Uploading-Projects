## Sticker Separator

Cause I hate having the slots without the defining feature of them, actually removing the sticker

Both normal and inverted are in the set/photos. The inverse causes the cart to roll the least while having less control while the normal one is the most controlled one.

Normal dispenses stickers towards the round bar and starts in the bottom slot.

Inverse dispense towards the tape cutter and start at the top.

#### Required Hardware

- Double sided tape, preferably 3M, used the 0.5"×18ft 5925 tape in particular.

#### Optional Hardware | Seems like the slot is enough

- 4x M4 spring set screw, set to barely have tension on a blank backing.
