When using a 1lb propane refill kit, having a 15lb tank upside down is natural... Right??

As a house hold we bought a 1lb propane refill set, nice shhhtuff lol. Wasn't wanting to drag things outside to fill takes, so made a hook and holder to hang it

The pins should be strong enough to put on the ground, didn't want to cut the rope by tying it off to the tank and it's constant length.

### Required Hardware

- 1 M5 Nut
- 1 M5 Screw ~14 mm
- 5 Length of strongish rope

### Installation Guide

1. Print parts
2. Putting the nut into place, glueing it in would be reccommended
3. Put on tank and use screw to hold it into place
4. Tie one side of 4 of the rope onto each pin.
5. Put the handle at a comfortable distance, tying each of them off while doing so
