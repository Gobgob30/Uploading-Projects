Simple Sheath for the Victorinox 5.6603.15

Has about 15 different "angles", higher angle the tighter the fit along the blade.

Angle I used was: .07 deg | Kinda of a tight fit

Recommended: .5 - .7 deg | Why I recommend going lower than .07 degs
